=== Meister Promotions ===
Requires PHP: 8.1
Requires Plugins: woocommerce
Stable tag: 1.0.0

Native merchandise coupons, automatic selected-product bundles, email-bound welcome issuance and durable headless checkout attempts. Campaigns are disabled by default. WooCommerce → Meister Promotions manages bundles and the future newsletter template; Marketing → Coupons manages ordinary codes and attribution.

Installation alone does not enable the new storefront checkout. Follow the operations guide for backend/proxy authentication, receiver isolation, reservation/reconciliation health and sandbox verification. No newsletter sending or first-purchase restriction is implemented. Delivery is excluded from all discounts. No uninstall handler removes orders or issuance history.
